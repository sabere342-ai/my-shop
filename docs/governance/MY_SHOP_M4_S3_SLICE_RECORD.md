## 3. Governing decisions taken inside authorized scope

1. **The average lives on the level, the snapshot lives on the movement (§30.2).** The engine stores `average_cost_minor` on `stock_levels` (per variant + location) and `unit_cost_minor` on every movement — the *permanent* record of what that movement actually used. No new averaging table was invented: the plan's §30.2 names the level as the recalculation point, and the fold writes both facts in one transaction.
2. **The engine is a pure function over locked facts (ADR-017 posture).** `costing.domain.ts` performs no I/O: it answers given the locked, current level facts the service reads inside the movement transaction. Serialization rides M4-S2's variant-row `FOR UPDATE` sequence point, so two racing cost-bearing submissions can never interleave two folds of one basis (proven by the scenario-G race).
3. **The fold has exactly one rounding point (§30.6).** `new_value = old_value + received · cost`, then one division under the organization's `rounding_mode` — read from the typed settings row *inside* the transaction, defaulting `HALF_UP` for an organization with no settings row. The tie is exercised on the real database: HALF_UP rounds 2.5 away to 3, HALF_EVEN to 2, and the settings row is upserted and restored inside the case itself.
4. **Deductions never restate (§30.4).** A consumption snapshots the current average verbatim; a plain incoming adjustment costs at the current average. Historical COGS is therefore never re-derived by later arithmetic — the value identity `quantity × average` is exact integer multiplication, witnessed per fold by the valuation read.
5. **REVALUATION is the only retroactive act (§30.5/§30.8), and its delta is structurally zero.** The canonicalizer refuses a non-zero REVALUATION delta (`400 VALIDATION_FAILED`) *before* any connection, the service refuses a restatement of uncosted stock (`409 INVENTORY_COST_BASIS_REQUIRED`), and the movement row carries only its own new average — the acquisition snapshots in the cost-snapshot ledger are never rewritten (asserted by the scenario-F/H comparisons on the real database).
6. **The wire cost is part of the movement fact — when the offer names one.** `isSameFact` compares the offered acquisition cost for every cost-imposing cause and for any offer that carries a cost; a bare MANUAL_ADJUSTMENT's snapshot cost is the engine's own derivation, so a bare retry replays regardless of what the engine derived then. The economic content is preserved exactly: a cost-bearing retry against a derived movement is *identical* if and only if the offered cost equals the derived snapshot (the folds agree), else the ledger answers the contradiction.
7. **A cost-bearing MANUAL_ADJUSTMENT folds like a receipt (§30.3).** Admitted with an offered cost as an inclusion — the same fold + snapshot path; a consumption with an offered cost is wire-refused (`400`), because the wire cannot re-cost what the engine's average owns.
8. **Permission escalation is controller-final and cause-shaped (§38.5).** The route declares `inventory.adjust`; `requiredCostingPermissions(fromCanonicalizedOffer)` widens to `inventory.receive` + `catalog.cost.edit` for PURCHASE_RECEIPT and to `catalog.cost.edit` for OPENING_BALANCE/REVALUATION/any offered cost — resolved through the real `AuthorizationService.resolveForMembership`, so the escalation matrix is exportable and unit-provable against the production decorators.
9. **The view faces stay cost-free.** `levels` and `movements` carry no cost member on the wire (the walkthrough asserts the JSON contains none); the §30.2 numbers answer only behind `catalog.cost.edit` (`GET valuation`, `GET cost-snapshots`), with uncosted lines honestly `null`, counted, and never folded into the total.
10. **Defence at three depths, carried forward.** (a) the service's fold inside `withTenantTransaction`; (b) M4-S2's transaction-wide `FOR UPDATE` sequence point; (c) migration 0015's CHECKs for a writer that bypasses the service (stock without a basis exists only at quantity zero; a basis is bounded) — each 23513 answer asserted on the real database.

**Wire-shape and schema-agreement decisions worth recording:**

1. **Money and quantities are decimal strings end to end** (§30.1): `unitCostMinor` rides the exact non-negative integer-string grammar (`/^\d{1,12}$/`, restated by `COST_WIRE_PATTERN`); the valuation and snapshot DTOs answer strings; no `number` ever holds a monetary value in the engine.
2. **The 0015 CHECKs are migration-only.** `prisma migrate diff` cannot see a CHECK expression, and M4-S2's record already learned what a schema/migration mismatch costs: `average_cost_minor` itself is the only thing the schema declares beyond the index, and the probe exits 0 on the final tree.
3. **The replay/contradiction cost rule is derivation-aware** (decision 3-6) — recorded here because it refines M4-S2's `isSameFact` without weakening a single predecessor refusal: every different fact the wire can name still answers the contradiction, and the walk of the ordering is asserted by scenarios D and F.
